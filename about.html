About Us

We are a bridge between worlds — science and spirit, research and tradition, past and future.

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding our work to preserve knowledge, biodiversity, and regenerative practices.

Our Mission

Raíces Vivas works to preserve ancestral plant knowledge and support its sharing in ways that benefit local and broader communities. We partner with existing gardens and local networks, providing resources, infrastructure, and ethical frameworks to strengthen their capacity while ensuring respectful, reciprocal collaboration.

Our Vision

We envision a network of thriving Amazonian gardens, where communities, researchers, and institutions collaborate to preserve biodiversity, safeguard traditional knowledge, and create sustainable, living models for conservation, learning, and cultural exchange.

Our Purpose

What we stand for and how we bring it to life through partnerships and action.

Values

  • Regeneration: We support ecological and cultural systems that thrive in balance.
  • Knowledge Sovereignty: Communities retain control over their ancestral knowledge.
  • Ethical Science: Research is conducted respectfully, transparently, and collaboratively.
  • Collaboration: We believe in co-creating solutions with local and global partners.
  • Accessibility: Knowledge and resources are shared openly and inclusively.

Approach

We collaborate closely with existing gardens, nurseries, and local networks, providing practical infrastructure, ethical frameworks, and resources to strengthen their capacity. Our support helps gardens thrive sustainably while ensuring secure access for researchers, educators, and knowledge-keepers. By co-designing projects with local communities, we foster partnerships that respect ancestral knowledge, promote regenerative practices, and create lasting models for conservation, education, and research across the Amazon.

About the funder

 I’m Andrea Chávez Muñoz, a Peruvian computational bioengineer who believes that science and tradition can grow stronger together. My path has taken me from research labs in Boston and Switzerland, where I worked on biological systems and behavioral studies, to farms across France, where I learned from people whose hands and intuition guide the land.

Growing up, we would visit Yurimaguas every few years, a gateway to the Amazon where my father’s roots lie. Though I grew up in the city, the forest has always felt like a quiet thread running through my life — one I began to follow more closely while spending time in Pucallpa, where my aunt runs a native plant nursery. There, I saw how quickly plant knowledge can disappear, and how alive it becomes when shared.

Raíces Vivas grew from that realization — a space to unite the precision of science with the wisdom of those who live in harmony with the forest. Through it, I hope to help safeguard the Amazon’s biodiversity and the ancestral wisdom that lives within it.
