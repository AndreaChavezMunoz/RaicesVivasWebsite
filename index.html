Our Vision

Through ethical collaboration, we aim to protect medicinal plants, document ancestral wisdom, and inspire new generations to live in harmony with the forest.We believe that true preservation begins with connection.
Our vision is to build a living network of Amazonian gardens — spaces where science and tradition meet, where knowledge flows both ways, and where communities lead the path to regeneration.

What We Do

Garden Partnerships 

We support and collaborate with existing Amazonian gardens, providing training, infrastructure, and visibility in exchange for shared access for research and education.

Knowledge & Research

We build bridges between scientists and indigenous knowledge holders through ethical frameworks that ensure community ownership and fair benefit-sharing.

Sustainable Infrastructure  

We co-design low-impact spaces — earthship-inspired housing, seed banks, and cultural centers — that serve as living models of regenerative living.

Why It Matters

The Amazon is disappearing — not only in trees and rivers, but in memory.
With each elder that passes, an entire library of plant wisdom risks being lost.
Our work helps safeguard this knowledge, ensuring the stories and practices that connect people to the forest endure.
